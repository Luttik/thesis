01

The storyline

This paragraph is the chapter. Every section opener, every transition, and the figure should be a restatement of one of these four sentences. If a passage doesn’t serve one of them, it gets merged or cut.

Value from agentic AI does not arrive with the technology — it is produced by a chain of managerial interventions. Managers first observe external conditions — AI’s rapid progression, market pressure from competitors and AI-using consumers, and vendor roadmaps — that build the impulse to act. They then turn inward and navigate, leverage, and reshape internal conditions: organizational capacity, leadership, technical resources, and governance. On that foundation they guide and experiment with adoption across a small set of high-value use cases, configured through tool calling and personalization. Finally, value must be mediated, not harvested: the same use case is transformative in one organization and fails in another, and the difference is the conditions and behaviors above — sharpest in the paradoxes, where the risks created by agentic AI are themselves managed with agentic AI.

Use sentence 1 as the new chapter intro’s thesis. Sentences 2–5 become the opening lines of 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, and 4.4 respectively — each section starts by locating itself in the chain (“Having observed these pressures, managers turn inward…”).

Act 2 · §4.2

Act 3 · §4.3

Act 4 · §4.4

Mediating & obtaining value creation

The payoff: benefits, sacrifices, risks — and why identical use cases diverge (Table 4 is the climax).
