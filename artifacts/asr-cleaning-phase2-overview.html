Cleaning pipeline (10 steps)

Hyphen restarts→ Short -- restarts→ Word -- word→ Phrase -- overlap→ Phrase , repeat→ Phrase space repeat→ Word , repeat→ Function-word doubles→ Long -- pause→ Literal fixes

Phase 2 patterns (new)

8. Phrase repeated with a space (not a comma)

Same stutter as phase 1, but the ASR inserted a space instead of a comma. Works for English and Dutch.

Beforein the in the line … what are the what are the epics … als ik als ik mijn … as a as a company
Afterin the line … what are the epics … als ik mijn … as a company

9. Overlapping restart with --

When the speaker restarts and the text after -- shares a prefix with the text before it.

Beforeit's not the-- it's not a … it's not the-- it's not an … you-- you guide
Afterit's not a … it's not an … you guide

Unrelated pairs like only-- that's or help-- you are left unchanged when there is no shared prefix.

Still left alone

  • Emphatic doubles — yeah, yeah, very, very, just, just (~112 comma repeats)
  • Trailing hyphens at turn ends — but-, um- (continued speech)
  • Abandoned starts — what are the-..., uh-... (~38; needs manual judgment)
  • Non-overlapping -- — e.g. only-- that's (~50 remaining)
  • Anonymisation tags — [product], [company], [Them]
  • Comma density from ASR chunking — not normalised (would change voice)
