Thesis · §5.1 Contributions to theory · reconsideration

Where do our key contributions actually lie?

The material in §5.1.2–§5.1.4 is strong and well-grounded — what's drifted is the framing. Since §5.1.1 became the "continuation of digital transformation, intensified" section, it now overlaps the three that follow, they still carry dropped scaffolding (dynamic capabilities, imported theory), and they read as a flat list ("a second… third… fourth contribution") rather than one argument.

Recommendation in one line

Keep the contributions, but organise them as a single coherent claim about how value creation changes under agentic AI — a where / who / what triad — and pull the sharpest, most novel finding (the governance inversion) out of where it's currently buried.

WHERE value is made → the configuration / harness WHO makes it & the limit → the manager WHAT value becomes → an entangled portfolio

02

What's actually in the data (the raw contributions)

Stripped of framing, here is the distinctive, Chapter-4-grounded material — and a verdict on each.

Value is produced in the configuration — the harness
Comparable use cases diverged with the configuration around the model (data access, memory, tools, guardrails, prompt) and the conditions around it — not with the model itself.
Evidence: Table 4 (§4.4.4) · harness (§4.3.2) · data-readiness (§4.2.3)
Keep · core
The manager is the decisive variable — a steering repertoire
Managers reshape, leverage, or navigate conditions; value tracks what they do, not what they buy. The AI champion (technical + business fluency) converts leadership backing into change.
Evidence: §4.2 conditions & behaviors · AI champion (§4.2.2)
Keep
…and managerial agency has a hard boundary — the governance inversion
Managers reshape what they own but navigate what they don't; governance has flipped from the literature's classic enabler to the main inhibitor, and no one reshaped it.
Evidence: §4.2.3–4.2.4 · navigation bias · governance as top obstacle
Promote
Value is an entangled portfolio, governed recursively
Benefits, sacrifices, and risks are produced together; the same technology governs its own risks — AI governs AI (brand/evaluator agents). Value destruction is actively managed, not just avoided.
Evidence: §4.4 (portfolio) · §4.5 (paradoxes)
Keep
Managerial demands intensify vs. prior DT waves
Speed of progression breeds resistance ("it goes so fast that they get overwhelmed"), so the managerial behaviors that mattered in earlier digital transformation matter more now.
Evidence: §4.1.1 · cross-cut with Enholm/Vidal/Gebauer
Fold into 5.1.1
Practice lens / value co-creation theory (Orlikowski; Vargo & Lusch)
Currently used to underwrite the configuration-in-use claim.
Status: imported theory; the grounded finding stands on its own
Trim to 1 line

Recommended structure — a where / who / what triad

One spine: agentic AI doesn't change that value is organizational (that's §5.1.1, the continuation) — it changes where value is made, who makes it, and what it becomes. Three sharp contributions, each clearly beyond the DT baseline, none leaning on dropped theory.

5.1.2 · Where

Value lives in the configuration, not the tool

The agentic-specific complementary asset is the harness plus the conditions around it. Value is read off the configuration, not the model (Table 4).
