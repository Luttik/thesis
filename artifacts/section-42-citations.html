Section 4.2 — Academic literature reference

Papers for use in the Discussion chapter when interpreting section 4.2 findings. Not cited in the Findings chapter itself. BibTeX keys match references.bib.

These papers were identified via web search (June 2026). Citation counts were not available at retrieval time. Verify DOIs and page ranges before final submission.
4.2.1

Capacity to change — Resistance, learning & experimentation

Consequence of Resistance to Change on AI Readiness: Mediating–Moderating Role of Task-oriented Leadership and High-Performance Work System in the Hospitality Sector
Li, C., Ashraf, S. F., Amin, S., & Safdar, M. N.  •  SAGE Open, 13(4)  •  2023  •  DOI: 10.1177/21582440231217731
Li, C., Ashraf, S. F., Amin, S., & Safdar, M. N. (2023). Consequence of resistance to change on AI readiness: Mediating–moderating role of task-oriented leadership and high-performance work system in the hospitality sector. SAGE Open, 13(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440231217731
Supports the finding that resistance to change directly reduces organizational AI readiness. Links to Interviewees 12, 13 on organizational resistance.
Moving beyond conventional resistance and resistors: an integrative review of employee resistance to digital transformation
Cieslak, V., & Valor, C.  •  Cogent Business & Management, 12(1)  •  2024  •  DOI: 10.1080/23311975.2024.2442550
Cieslak, V., & Valor, C. (2024). Moving beyond conventional resistance and resistors: An integrative review of employee resistance to digital transformation. Cogent Business & Management, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.1080/23311975.2024.2442550
Integrative review of 63 papers on employee resistance to digital transformation. Useful framing for the finding that resistance is multifaceted (psychological, political, cultural) — not just individuals being difficult.
Developing industrial AI capabilities: An organisational learning perspective
Ritala, P., Aaltonen, P., Ruokonen, M., & Nemeh, A.  •  Technovation, 138, 103120  •  2024  •  DOI: 10.1016/j.technovation.2024.103120
Ritala, P., Aaltonen, P., Ruokonen, M., & Nemeh, A. (2024). Developing industrial AI capabilities: An organisational learning perspective. Technovation, 138, 103120. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.technovation.2024.103120
Distinguishes explorative vs. exploitative learning in AI capability-building. Supports the finding that experimentation (section 4.2.1) is the primary mechanism through which capacity to change is built. Links to Interviewees 6, 13, 17.
4.2.2

Available resources — Data infrastructure & organizational capabilities

Organizational Capabilities for AI Implementation—Coping with Inscrutability and Data Dependency in AI
Weber, M., Engert, M., Schaffer, N., Weking, J., & Krcmar, H.  •  Information Systems Frontiers, 25(4), 1549–1569  •  2023  •  DOI: 10.1007/s10796-022-10297-y
Weber, M., Engert, M., Schaffer, N., Weking, J., & Krcmar, H. (2023). Organizational capabilities for AI implementation—Coping with inscrutability and data dependency in AI. Information Systems Frontiers, 25(4), 1549–1569. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10796-022-10297-y
Identifies specific organizational capabilities required for AI: data quality, human skills, IT infrastructure, and business–AI department relationships. Directly maps to the data infrastructure and systems-thinking findings (Interviewees 2, 5, 13).
4.2.3

Leadership & Governance — Champions, backing & bureaucracy

Adoption and integration of AI in organizations: a systematic review of challenges and drivers towards future directions of research
Romeo, E., & Lacko, J.  •  Kybernetes, 55(3), 1286–1307  •  2026  •  DOI: 10.1108/K-07-2024-2002
Romeo, E., & Lacko, J. (2026). Adoption and integration of AI in organizations: A systematic review of challenges and drivers towards future directions of research. Kybernetes, 55(3), 1286–1307. https://doi.org/10.1108/K-07-2024-2002
Broad systematic review (196 papers) covering leadership, governance, and organizational drivers of AI adoption. Useful for situating the leadership and champion findings within the wider AI adoption literature.
