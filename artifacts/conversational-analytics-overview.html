Building the conversational-analytics story

Core claim: agentic AI is collapsing the distance between marketers and their data, shifting the act from writing queries to asking questions. Whether that value lands depends on the organization's data foundation, and where it holds, the payoff is a shortened learning loop with organizational consequences.

The story arc

Four beats. Beat 3 is the hinge: it explains why the same models gave opposite outcomes across participants, which is already the pivot of the current §4.3.1 draft.

BEAT 1

Name the pattern

From querying to conversing: marketers "talk to data."

BEAT 2

The SQL transition

Two postures: write SQL by hand vs. wire the warehouse into the model.

BEAT 3

The precondition

Data governance decides success, and explains the divergence.

BEAT 4

The payoff

Time to insight compresses; the loop shortens; more experiments, more wins.
